PART 1
Introduction & Interview: Cities
The examiner will ask you general questions about yourself and a range of familiar topics. This part lasts 4–5 minutes.
Listen to each question and respond naturally. Aim for 2–3 sentences per answer. You may record your responses below.
1.What city do you live in or come from? Tell me a little about it.
2.Do you prefer living in a city or in the countryside? Why?
3.Is there anything you would like to change about your city?
4.Do you think cities are becoming more or less pleasant places to live? Why?
PART 2
Individual Long Turn
You have 1 minute to prepare before speaking for 1–2 minutes on the topic below. The examiner may ask one or two questions after you finish.
Describe a time when you read or heard something that you thought was not true.
You should say:
  • where you read or heard it
  • what you read or heard
  • why you thought it was not true
  • and how you felt about it
Speak for 1–2 minutes. The examiner will tell you when to stop.
Take 1 minute to make notes if needed, then record your response below.
PART 3
Two-way Discussion: Honesty
The examiner will ask you more abstract and analytical questions related to the topic in Part 2. This part lasts 4–5 minutes.
Develop your ideas fully. Try to give opinions, justify them, and explore different perspectives.
Honesty and children
1.How important is it for children to learn to be honest?
2.Should children always be told the truth? Are there any situations where it might be acceptable to tell children things that are not completely true?
3.How do you think children come to understand the difference between right and wrong?
Honesty and advertising
4.How honest do you think advertising is in your country?
5.Do you think the government should do more to control the truth in advertising?
6.Are there any situations where it is acceptable for companies to present information that is not completely accurate?
